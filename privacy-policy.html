Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the following mobile applications developed by Max ("we", "our", or "us"), available on Google Play:

  • Urlaubstage-Planer (Vacation Planner) — package: de.smcode.vacationplaner
  • Wochenbericht — package: de.ms.wochenbericht

We respect your privacy. Our applications are designed to work on your device without collecting, storing on our servers, or transmitting any personal or usage data.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our applications do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Contacts, photos, or other content from your device
  • Payment or financial information

Local Data on Your Device

Our apps store data locally on your device so they can function offline:

  • Urlaubstage-Planer saves vacation entries, categories, and settings locally using on-device storage (Hive).
  • Wochenbericht saves work time records, project sites, and settings locally using on-device storage (SQLite).

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it (for example, exporting a PDF or a JSON backup file). We do not have access to this information and it is never sent to our servers or any external services automatically.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. Our apps do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected or transmitted, our apps are safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: smcodeapp@gmail.com

Политика конфиденциальности (RU)

Эта политика распространяется на приложения Urlaubstage-Planer и Wochenbericht.

Мы не собираем, не храним на серверах и не передаём никакие персональные или технические данные. Данные приложений (отпуска, рабочие записи, настройки) сохраняются только на вашем устройстве.

Данные покидают устройство только если вы сами экспортируете PDF или JSON-резервную копию. Мы не используем аналитику, рекламные SDK или сторонние сервисы отслеживания.
